# Code of Conduct

We want Logic Map to be a welcoming, technically rigorous open-source project.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate with respect and assume good intent;
- critique ideas and code without attacking people;
- welcome different experience levels and backgrounds;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted personal attention;
- protect private or proprietary information shared during debugging.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's moderation contact. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and without retaliation.
